# University Student Handbook & Campus Policy

## Section 1: General Code of Conduct
Students are expected to adhere to the university honor code. Respect for faculty, staff, and fellow peers is mandatory in all academic and campus environments.

## Section 2: Attendance Requirement
The attendance requirement for all undergraduate programs is 75% in every registered theory and laboratory course. Students with attendance below 75% will not be permitted to appear for the end-semester examinations unless an official medical leave application approved by the Dean of Student Affairs has been submitted within 5 working days of absence.

## Section 3: Grading Scheme and SGPA
The university adopts a 10-point credit-based grading scale:
- Grade O (Outstanding): 10 grade points (Marks >= 90)
- Grade A+ (Excellent): 9 grade points (Marks 80-89)
- Grade A (Very Good): 8 grade points (Marks 70-79)
- Grade B+ (Good): 7 grade points (Marks 60-69)
- Grade B (Above Average): 6 grade points (Marks 50-59)
- Grade C (Average): 5 grade points (Marks 40-49)
- Grade F (Fail): 0 grade points (Marks < 40)

To be eligible for degree conferment, a student must secure a minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 5.0.

## Section 4: Library and Digital Resources
The Central Library is open on all working days from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Each undergraduate student may borrow up to 4 books for a maximum duration of 14 calendar days. A late return fee of Rs. 5 per day per book is levied thereafter. Digital repository credentials will be provided during freshman orientation.

## Section 5: Hostel and Curfew Guidelines
Hostel residents must report inside the hostel premises by 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on Saturdays. Night-out permissions must be applied through the digital student portal at least 24 hours in advance and require parent SMS verification.
